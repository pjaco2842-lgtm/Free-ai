# What the 25K rules make possible

Arithmetic, not a backtest — generated by `trading/feasibility.py`. One MES micro, ES ATR(14) = 70 pts, risk = $350 at 1.0 ATR, round-trip cost $4.98.

## The win rate each target needs

| target | win needed | $/win | $/loss | $/trade @60% | @70% | @78% | @85% |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.2R | 84.5% | $70 | $350 | $-103 | $-61 | $-27 | $+2 |
| 0.3R | 78.0% | $105 | $350 | $-82 | $-36 | $-0 | $+32 |
| 0.5R | 67.6% | $175 | $350 | $-40 | $+13 | $+55 | $+91 |
| 1.0R | 50.7% | $350 | $350 | $+65 | $+135 | $+191 | $+240 |
| 1.5R | 40.6% | $525 | $350 | $+170 | $+258 | $+328 | $+389 |
| 2.0R | 33.8% | $700 | $350 | $+275 | $+380 | $+464 | $+538 |

Read the last four columns against the second. A 0.2R target wins 85% of the time and still loses money at an 85% win rate: one loss costs five wins. The high-win-rate books the desk keeps asking for are the ones with no expectancy. 0.5R needs **67.6%**, which is the number the whole live book rests on.

## Size required to make the three-day number

| $/session wanted | micros at 0.5R, 1 trade/leg | risk if every leg stops | share of the $1,000 buffer |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| $200 | 4 | $1400 | 140% |
| $300 | 6 | $2100 | 210% |
| $417 | 8 | $2800 | 280% |
| $500 | 9 | $3150 | 315% |

$417 a session is the three-day pace. At 0.5R and the desk's own 78% win rate that is $55/trade, so it needs ~8 micros. 8 legs stopping is $2800 against a $1000 buffer: one ordinary bad session ends the evaluation, and there is one account.

## What each ATR stop costs on one MES micro

| stop | risk | share of buffer | stop is legal on 25K |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 0.50 ATR | $175 | 18% | yes |
| 0.75 ATR | $262 | 26% | yes |
| 1.00 ATR | $350 | 35% | yes |
| 1.50 ATR | $525 | 52% | one trade, no margin for error |
| 2.00 ATR | $700 | 70% | one trade, no margin for error |
| 2.50 ATR | $875 | 88% | one trade, no margin for error |

This is why 2.0 and 2.5 ATR are not available on a 25K however good they look: $700 and $875 are 80-100% of the whole risk budget in one trade. On a 50K ($2,000 buffer) the same stops are 40-50%, which is the real argument for the 50K, not a better signal.
