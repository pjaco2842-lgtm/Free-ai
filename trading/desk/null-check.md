# Does the high win rate survive the drift control?

Random long entries on the same weekdays, same stop, same target, 200 draws. Alpha = rule − null. Bar for two settings: t > 2.12.

| setting | trades | win | $/trade | null win | null $/trade | alpha | t | clears? |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| NQ=F 78.5% win, 0.1R target, 2.0 ATR stop | 1273 | 78.5% | +14.08 | 77.7% | -1.56 | +15.64 | +2.00 | no |
| CL=F 82.8% win, 0.1R target, 1.0 ATR stop | 1319 | 82.8% | -16.80 | 80.3% | -22.64 | +5.84 | +2.01 | no |

The null's win rate is the answer: most of a high win rate is the drift every long position collects, not the rule.
